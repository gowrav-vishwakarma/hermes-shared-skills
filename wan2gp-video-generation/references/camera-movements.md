# Camera Movements for LTX-2.3 Video Prompts

Catalog of cinematic camera movements with prompt-ready phrasing for LTX-2.3. Each entry gives the exact words to drop into a video prompt. Adapted from [AIShotStudio's 42 Camera Movements](https://aishotstudio.com/42-camera-movements-ai-prompts/) and filtered for movements LTX-2.3 reliably interprets.

**Usage:** Copy the **prompt phrase** directly into your video prompt at the point where you want the movement to occur. Combine with acting beats and dialogue segmentation per the main [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

---

## Dolly / Track

Physical camera moves forward, backward, or laterally along a path.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Slow Dolly In | `Slow dolly in, camera moves slowly forward toward the subject` | Build intimacy or tension; draw the viewer into a character's emotion |
| Slow Dolly Out | `Slow dolly out, camera moves slowly backward away from the subject` | End a scene, reveal isolation, or create emotional distance |
| Fast Dolly In | `Fast dolly in, camera moves rapidly forward toward the subject, urgent motion` | Shock, sudden realization, or dramatic confrontation |
| Reveal from Behind | `Camera slides laterally from behind foreground object to reveal the scene` | Dramatic scene reveals; transition from mystery to clarity |
| Through Shot | `Fly through, camera moves through an opening into the scene` | Enter a new environment; portal or doorway reveals |
| Leading Shot | `Leading shot, camera moves backward matching the subject's speed` | Face-on dialogue while walking; subject advances toward viewer |
| Following Shot | `Following shot, camera follows behind the subject matching speed` | Chase, journey, or pursuit; build momentum behind a character |
| Side Tracking | `Side tracking, camera trucks alongside the subject` | Walking conversations; parallel motion conveying shared pace |
| Truck Left | `Truck left, camera moves sideways on a track to the left` | Lateral reveal; slide to expose a new element on the right |
| Truck Right | `Truck right, camera moves sideways on a track to the right` | Lateral reveal; slide to expose a new element on the left |

## Zoom / Lens

Optical changes -- the camera body stays put, the lens does the work.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Smooth Optical Zoom In | `Smooth optical zoom in, lens magnifies subject, camera stays stationary` | Gradually isolate a detail without physical camera motion |
| Smooth Optical Zoom Out | `Smooth optical zoom out, lens widens, background becomes blurry` | Pull back to reveal context while softening the background |
| Snap Zoom (Crash Zoom) | `Snap zoom, crash zoom, rapid zoom directly into the subject` | Comedic punch, sudden focus on a critical detail |
| Extreme Macro Zoom | `Extreme macro zoom, zoom transition from subject to micro details of surface` | Texture reveals -- skin, fabric, materials, natural surfaces |
| Cosmic Hyper Zoom | `Cosmic hyper zoom, fast zoom transition from extreme wide view down to macro level` | Scale transitions; universe-to-atom or landscape-to-detail |
| Vertigo Effect (Zolly) | `Vertigo effect, dolly zoom, camera moves backward while zooming in, background expands` | Disorientation, psychological dread, world-shifting realization |
| Rack Focus | `Rack focus, focus shifts from the foreground object to the background subject` | Redirect attention between two depth planes mid-shot |
| Reveal from Blur | `Rack focus, start completely out of focus, slowly pull focus until sharp` | Dream-to-reality transition; opening a scene with mystery |
| Fisheye Lens | `Fisheye lens, extreme wide-angle distortion, circular frame` | Surreal POV, peephole view, exaggerated close-range distortion |

## Pan / Tilt

Rotational moves on a fixed camera position -- the camera swivels but doesn't travel.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Tilt Up | `Tilt up, camera pans vertically up from bottom to top` | Reveal height, grandeur, or a character's full stature |
| Tilt Down | `Tilt down, camera pans vertically down from top to bottom` | Descend from sky to ground; reveal what's below |
| Whip Pan | `Whip pan, camera whips violently to the side with extreme directional motion blur` | Snap transitions, comedic timing, frantic energy |

---

## Orbit / Arc

Camera circles around the subject, partially or fully.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Orbit 180 | `Orbit 180, camera moves in a half-circle around the subject` | Hero reveal; show a character from multiple angles in one shot |
| Fast 360 Orbit | `Fast 360 orbit, camera spins rapidly 360 degrees around the subject` | High-energy emphasis; music-video drama; power moment |
| Slow Cinematic Arc | `Slow cinematic arc, camera moves in a wide curve to reveal side profile` | Contemplative reveal; beauty shot; fashion editorial |

## Crane / Pedestal

Vertical elevation changes -- the camera rises or descends.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Crane Up | `Crane up, camera lifts high into the air` | Triumphant reveal; escalate from intimate to epic scale |
| Crane Down | `Crane down, camera descends slowly to the subject` | Arrival; settling into a scene; descend from god's-eye to ground |
| Pedestal Up | `Pedestal up, camera rises vertically straight up from waist to eye level` | Gradual character reveal from body to face |
| Pedestal Down | `Pedestal down, camera lowers vertically straight down` | Shift focus downward; reveal what's at ground level |

## Drone / Aerial

High-altitude or large-scale sweeping camera work.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Drone Fly Over | `Drone fly over, high altitude flight moving forward over the landscape` | Establishing shots; travel sequences; scale of environment |
| Epic Drone Reveal | `Epic drone reveal, rising and tilting down to reveal the scene` | Grand opening; reveal a location for the first time |
| Large Scale Drone Orbit | `Large scale drone orbit, massive sweeping circle around the landscape` | Awe-inspiring environment showcase; epic scope |
| Top Down (God's Eye) | `Top down shot, camera pointing straight down, slow twist` | Abstract composition; surveillance aesthetic; overhead reveal |
| FPV Drone Dive | `FPV drone dive, aggressive diving motion down a vertical structure` | Adrenaline; extreme sports aesthetic; vertiginous descent |

## Stylized / Effect

Specialized moves that break conventional camera grammar for dramatic or artistic impact.

| Movement | Prompt phrase | When to use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Handheld Documentary | `Handheld camera, shaky motion, natural movement, documentary style` | Raw authenticity; vlog feel; emotional intensity |
| Dutch Angle (Roll) | `Dutch angle, camera roll, tilted sideways on Z-axis` | Unease, instability, psychological tension |
| POV Walk | `POV walk, first person camera moving forward with bobbing motion` | Immersive exploration; horror; first-person narrative |
| Over the Shoulder | `Over the shoulder shot, camera mounted behind subject A framing subject B` | Dialogue scenes; establish spatial relationship between two people |
| Hyperlapse | `Hyperlapse, camera moves forward rapidly, time accelerated, fast motion, light trails` | Time passage; city energy; day-to-night transitions |
| Barrel Roll | `Barrel roll, camera spins 360 degrees clockwise while moving forward, disorienting` | Surreal, dreamlike, or disorienting sequences |
| Bullet Time | `Bullet time, frozen moment, ultra slow motion, camera orbit` | Freeze a peak-action moment; dramatic emphasis on a single instant |
| Worm's Eye Tracking | `Worm's eye view, low angle tracking, camera moves along the ground looking up` | Power, dominance, towering scale; hero entrance from below |

## Combining Movements

The most dramatic shots layer a camera move with an acting beat and a reveal. Examples:

**Dolly + Reveal:**
> A slow dolly-in past her shoulder reveals the nebula filling the viewport.

**Crane + Tilt:**
> Crane up, camera lifts high into the air, then tilts down to reveal the sprawling city below.

**Orbit + Rack Focus:**
> The camera arcs in a slow cinematic orbit around her, rack focus shifting from her face to the glowing skyline behind.

**Handheld + Snap Zoom:**
> Handheld camera, documentary style, suddenly snap zooms into his widening eyes.

Keep combinations to two moves per shot. Stacking three or more competing motions confuses the model.
