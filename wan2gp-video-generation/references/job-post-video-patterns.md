# Job Post / Recruitment Video Prompts

## When to use
The user wants to announce a job opening (Founder's Office, team hire, etc.) as a 20s Pixar-style video with their character directly addressing potential applicants.

## Prompt structure (3-part)

### 1. Scene Setup
```
INT. [LOCATION] — DAY. The character [position/action], [background elements]. [Lighting/atmosphere].
```

### 2. Opening Hook + Job Title (first 3 seconds)
Start with a loud, attention-grabbing declaration:
```
"WANTED — [Job Title]."
```

### 3. Candidate Requirements (spoken with escalating energy)
Describe the ideal candidate using direct address ("you"):
```
"You're [trait 1]. You [skill/ability]. You understand [domain] — and you're NOT shy about [action]."
```

### 4. Closing Call-to-Action
```
"You plan smart. You execute faster. If that's you — we need you on the team."
[Visual beat: points at camera, confident nod, camera pulls back]
```

## Complete prompt template
```
INT. [LOCATION] — DAY. The character [position] at/near [props], [background details]. [Lighting].

Camera [movement] as he looks [up/from work] and addresses the viewer with [energy/tone].

"WANTED — [JOB TITLE]."

He [small action — sets down pen, gestures], speaking with [enthusiasm/confidence/intensity].

"You're [adjective]. You [specific skill statement about knowing stuff]. You understand [numbers/operations/connections], [domain] — and you're NOT [shy/intimidated/afraid] about [outreach/action]."

He [body language — leans forward, gestures animatedly], speaking with [energy level].

"You plan smart. You execute faster. If that's you — [final statement].

He points directly at the camera with [challenge/confidence], then gives a confident nod as the camera [pulls back].

Audio: [music genre], warm [confident/energetic] voice, [ambient sound description].
```

## Script rules
- **Max 4-5 dialogue lines** (fits 20s comfortably)
- **Opening hook first** — "WANTED — [Title]" in first 3 seconds
- **Direct address** — use "you" throughout, never "we are hiring"
- **Specific traits** — "sharp," "not shy," "good with numbers," "connects people," "plans smart, executes fast"
- **Local preference mention** — if applicable, add inline (e.g., "Ahmedabad locals preferred")
- **No CTA after closing beat** — last line is the job announcement close; visual beat follows
- **No trailing dialogue** after the visual closing (LTX-2.3 reads prompt ONCE, trailing dialogue loops)

## Location ideas (generate separate anchors for)
- Premium office (glass desk, city skyline, golden hour)
- Co-working space (brick walls, plants, bean bags, string lights)
- Home office setup (desk with dual monitors, bookshelf, coffee mug)
- Outdoor café (table with laptop, city bustle background)

## LoRA recommendation
- Pixar_Toon at 1.0 for cleaner character (less distortion)
- Pixar_Toon at 1.5 for stronger cartoon/exaggerated look
- A/B test both weights for the same location

## Example (Founder's Office Associate — Ahmedabad)
```
INT. CREATIVE CO-WORKING SPACE — DAY. The character sits at a wooden table with a notebook and coffee mug, exposed brick wall behind, hanging plants and string lights visible. Natural sunlight streams through large windows.

Camera pushes in slowly as he looks up from his notebook and smiles at the viewer with genuine warmth.

"WANTED — Founder's Office Associate."

He sets down his pen and leans forward, speaking with genuine enthusiasm.

"You're sharp. You know social media like your own Instagram. You understand numbers, funnels, connecting people — and you're NOT shy about reaching out."

He gestures with both hands, animated and energetic.

"If you're from Ahmedabad — even better. You plan smart. You execute faster. If that's you — we need you on the team."

He points directly at the camera with a challenge in his eyes, then gives a confident nod as the camera pulls back slightly.

Audio: upbeat indie pop background music, warm confident male voice, co-working space ambience with soft chatter.
```
