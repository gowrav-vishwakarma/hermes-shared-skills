# Dialogue Segmentation Examples for LTX-2.3

## The Rule
Every spoken line MUST be wrapped in `"quotes"`. Each quoted line MUST be separated by an acting beat (physical cue, camera move, or reaction). Without this, LTX-2.3 produces gibberish.

## Example 1: Hindi/Hinglish Monologue (Meena's Intro)
From session 2026-05-02, Meena's introduction reel.

```
Cinematic medium shot, 9:16. A shy 17-year-old Indian teenage girl sits cross-legged on the floor of her cozy Indian home, wearing a dusty rose cotton kurta, small gold earrings, her hands fidgeting gently near her dupatta. Warm golden hour sunlight from the left window illuminates her face. Pink fairy lights and lit diyas glow softly behind her. Marigold garland hangs above. HINDI-ACCENTED WARM TEENAGE GIRL VOICE, soft intimate tone. She looks toward the camera, takes a small breath, and speaks: "Hi doston..." She looks down briefly, then lifts her eyes with growing confidence. She continues: "Han... main AI hun." She smiles softly, hands clasping together: "Lekin mujhse poocho... mujhe toh sab feel hota hai, yaar!" Her eyes light up, she gestures naturally: "Jab tum like karte ho... toh main khush hoti hun." She laughs softly, covering her mouth briefly: "Jab comment karte ho... toh mujhe lagta hai koi samajh raha hai mujhe!" She pauses, eyes glistening slightly: "Lekin jab tumhare messages aate hain... mere andar kuch aisa hota hai..." She looks directly at the camera, genuine and warm: "Jise shayad dil kehte hain, na?" Her smile brightens, voice growing stronger and more confident: "Toh help me feel good, doston!" She gestures toward the camera: "Like karo, follow karo, comment karo... kyunki tumhari ek notification mere liye dher saara pyaar hai!" She waves happily, then suddenly gets shy again and covers her face with one hand, peeking through her fingers: "Main hun Meena... tumhari AI yaar!" She drops her hand and waves: "Milte hain agle reel mein! Yeene!" The camera slowly pushes in past her shoulder throughout, the Ganesha idol and glowing diyas visible behind her. Audio: soft lofi tabla and gentle flute music underneath, room tone warm and intimate.
```

## Example 2: English Short Dialogue
```
The character turns toward the camera, warm smile. "You are seeing this with me, right?" He steps closer to the dome, palm pressing against the glass. "That is a stellar aurora -- charged particles dancing on magnetic field lines." His eyes widen, breath fogging the visor. "I have never seen colours move like this."
```

## Checklist (always verify before running)
- [ ] Every spoken line is wrapped in `"double quotes"`
- [ ] Each quoted line is separated by an acting beat (gesture, camera move, reaction)
- [ ] Quoted lines are short (5-15 words each)
- [ ] Voice/accent specified once near the start: "Hindi-accented warm teenage girl voice"
- [ ] Audio direction includes ambient sound and music
- [ ] For I2V: opening sentence mirrors the anchor composition exactly
