# Content-Type CTA Considerations

## When NOT to use CTA

Not all video content types require a Call-to-Action (CTA). The standard "hit like and follow" pattern is inappropriate for certain content categories.

### Pure Spiritual/Divine Content

**Examples**: Divine messages, faith-based teachings, bhajan narration, sacred storytelling

**Rule**: Keep it pure. No promotional CTA at the end. The message stands on its own.

**Why**: Adding "hit like and follow" breaks the spiritual atmosphere and undermines the sacred nature of the content. The divine character speaking from heaven should remain in character throughout.

**Session evidence (2026-05-12)**: User requested divine motivation video about faith and surrender. Initial plan included CTA "hit like and follow for more divine wisdom." User corrected: "do not use any CTA now.. its divine message, lets keep it that way only."

**Pattern**: Divine/spiritual content → NO CTA, just the sacred message

### Other Content Types Without CTA

**Deep research insights**: When sharing data-driven analysis or research findings, the value is in the insight itself. No need for CTA.

**Horror storytelling**: Horror builds atmosphere and tension. Ending with "hit like" breaks the tension and immersion.

**Mythological dialogue drama**: The story and drama are the focus. CTA interrupts the narrative flow.

### When CTA IS Appropriate

- **Character intros**: When introducing a new avatar/character
- **Educational content**: When teaching a skill or concept
- **Series continuations**: When the video is part of an ongoing series
- **Promotional content**: When the goal is engagement/growth

## Workflow adjustment

**Pre-flight checklist before adding CTA:**
1. What is the content type?
2. Is it pure spiritual/divine content? → NO CTA
3. Is it deep research/horror/mythology? → Consider NO CTA
4. Is it character intro/educational/promotional? → CTA appropriate
5. If in doubt, ASK the user before adding CTA

**Default behavior**: Do not assume CTA is required. Wait for user confirmation or content type that clearly needs it.
