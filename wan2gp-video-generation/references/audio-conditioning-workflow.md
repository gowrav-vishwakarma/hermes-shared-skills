# Audio Conditioning Without Motion Transfer

Use this when you want a character to move/dance to an audio track WITHOUT transferring pose/motion from a control video.

## When to Use

- You have an audio track (from a trend, a song, a recording) and want the character to respond to it
- You want the character to generate dance/free movement synced to the audio, not copy someone else's poses
- The trend-copy workflow (copy_trend.py + OVG) is too restrictive — you don't want pose transfer

## Workflow

### Step 1: Extract or provide audio

The trend download workflow already creates `trend_source_clean_control_audio.wav` in the post folder. Use this directly.

### Step 2: Generate anchor image

Create a dance pose anchor for your character using `generate_image_config.py`:

```bash
python3 "$PROFILE_SKILLS/wan2gp-image-generation/scripts/generate_image_config.py" \
    --prompt "Cinematic medium shot, 9:16. Young Indian teenage girl performing a dance pose — arms raised, one foot lifted, joyful expression..." \
    --image-refs "/path/to/character.png" \
    --output-filename dance_anchor \
    --output-dir "$POSTS_DIR/YYYY-MM-DD_dance_audio" \
    --aspect 9:16 \
    --seed 742981 \
    --run
```

### Step 3: Generate video config with audio conditioning

Use `--audio-guide` (NOT `--video-guide`) to condition the video on audio only:

```bash
python3 "$PROFILE_SKILLS/wan2gp-video-generation/scripts/generate_video_config.py" \
    --prompt "INT. STUDIO. The camera slowly pushes in as she breaks into dance. 'This one goes out to the ones who feel it' (voice warm, infectious energy) She moves fluidly — hip isolations, sharp arm accents, footwork crisp on the beat. Mid-bar: she hits a full turn, hair flying, eyes locked on the lens with a grin. 'When the bass drops, you don't think — you just move.' (laughing between counts) She freezes on the last beat, chest rising, breath catching, then smiles. Audio: heavy bass drops, crisp hi-hats, her voice breathing with the music, natural room reverb." \
    --image-start "/path/to/dance_anchor.jpg" \
    --audio-guide "/path/to/trend_audio.wav" \
    --output-filename dance_audio_reel \
    --output-dir "$POSTS_DIR/YYYY-MM-DD_dance_audio" \
    --aspect 9:16
```

This sets `audio_prompt_type: "A"` — the model conditions the video on the audio waveform. The character will move to the beat but generates its own movement (no pose transfer).

### Step 4: Launch wgp.py

```bash
cd "$WAN_APP_DIR" && \
  "$WAN_PYTHON" wgp.py \
  --process "$POSTS_DIR/YYYY-MM-DD_dance_audio/video_generation.json" \
  --output-dir "$POSTS_DIR/YYYY-MM-DD_dance_audio" \
  --attention sage2 --profile 4 --fp16
```

## audio_prompt_type modes

| Type | Flag | Behavior |
|------|------|----------|
| `A` | `--audio-guide <path>` | Condition video on audio file. Character generates free movement synced to audio. |
| `K` | Auto-set by copy_trend.py with `--audio-from-control-video` | Extract audio from control video. Use with OVG/PVG/DVG/EVG pose transfer. |
| (empty) | None | No audio conditioning. Video is silent. |

## Key Differences

| Feature | OVG + Audio (K) | Audio Conditioning (A) |
|---------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Movement source | From control video | Generated by model |
| Lip sync | Yes (from video) | Yes (from audio) |
| Choreography | Copies trend | Free/creative |
| Best for | Trend copies | Original dance to any audio |
